### Required upgrade steps for 0.4.1

This security patch changes workload admission and cleanup requirements. Updating
the operator alone does not protect existing Pods. Plan a maintenance window for
affected workloads and preserve the central lease store and fencing-token history.

1. Stop producers, pause/suspend managed targets, and drain their Pods and active
   Jobs. Confirm the processes have stopped; force deletion is not a substitute.
2. For operator-managed targets, delete the drained targets and their BerthLeases,
   allowing cleanup/finalizers to finish. Upgrade the CRD, operator chart and image
   together. Helm does not upgrade bundled CRDs automatically: apply the reviewed
   CRD explicitly. Enable `workloadManagement.enabled` and configure admission TLS
   through `injection.webhook.tls`; confirm the admission webhooks are ready. Create
   replacement BerthLeases before recreating their targets. Do not copy old status
   or UID annotations. Lease-only resources do not require target recreation.
3. For injected runtime workloads, upgrade **both** injector and `berth-acquire`
   images, then recreate Pods. Both probe and signal modes now require Berth's
   freshness liveness probe on every regular container. Remove conflicting
   liveness/startup probes or redesign application health checks; readiness probes
   remain available. Foreign init containers are not supported in runtime mode.
   Keep admission fail-closed. Finish upgrading every injector replica before
   relying on the new admission policy.
4. Replace slash-containing tenant identifiers with flat identifiers and update
   client holder roots. Upgrade every API-server replica before restarting those
   clients. Runtime holder identities now include the Pod UID; replacements must
   not reuse copied holder/token state.

Kubernetes-backed releases and garbage collection preserve tombstones and token
history while using a valid, omitted lease-duration field. Released leases remain
immediately reclaimable. All four runtime images use `v0.4.1` tags; packaged charts
use version `0.4.1` with `appVersion: v0.4.1` so default image references agree.

Rollback requires another full drain and recreation under the older contract.
Do not disable admission, remove finalizers or delete status while managed work
is active. Kubelet processing and termination grace still contribute to stopping
time; downstream fencing tokens remain necessary to reject stale writes strictly.
